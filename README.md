# 2022 US Airline Cancellations and Detroit Winter Routes

Exploratory analysis of **6,729,125 scheduled domestic flights** across **17 carriers** using 2022 Bureau of Transportation Statistics (BTS) On-Time Performance data.

## Questions

1. How did cancellation rates differ between airlines during a March 5–April 9 spring-break window?
2. Which destinations received the most Detroit (DTW) departures in winter, and how did predefined warm-weather markets compare with other months?

## Findings described in the final memo

Spring-break cancellation rates included **Spirit 8.5%**, **JetBlue 6.7%**, **Frontier 5.9%**, and **Allegiant 5.7%**. Five predefined warm-weather destinations represented **11.9%** of winter DTW departures versus **9.5%** in other months. The memo includes sensitivity analysis and appropriate uncertainty caveats.

These are **flight counts and proportions**, not passenger demand, airline revenue, or causal estimates.
